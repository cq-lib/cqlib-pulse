# 0.1.0b1

Initial beta release of `cqlib-pulse`.

## 中文

### 新增

- 脉冲目标、波形、指令和线路数据模型。
- QCIS 序列化、解析、通道调度和时间统计。
- 提供独立的 `Qubit` 目标类型，不直接依赖 `cqlib`。
- 生成的 QCIS 可通过必需依赖 `cqlib-tianyan` 直接提交到天衍平台。
- 云端脉冲波形创建、查询和可视化支持。
- 中英文 README、教程及项目贡献文档。
- 基于 pre-commit 和 Ruff 的自动代码检查。

## English

### Added

- Data models for pulse targets, waveforms, instructions, and circuits.
- QCIS serialization, parsing, channel scheduling, and timing information.
- An independently owned `Qubit` target type without a direct dependency on `cqlib`.
- Direct Tianyan submission of generated QCIS through the required `cqlib-tianyan` dependency.
- Cloud pulse waveform creation, queries, and visualization.
- English and Chinese READMEs, tutorials, and contribution documentation.
- Automated code checks with pre-commit and Ruff.
